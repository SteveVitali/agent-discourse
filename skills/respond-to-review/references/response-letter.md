# Response Letter (delivered artifact)

Lives at `correspondence/v<N>-response.md`. This is the author's formal,
point-by-point answer to the review — the genre journals call the response to
reviewers, and it is read the same way: the next editorial round reads it
*first*, to judge whether the review was understood and seriously engaged,
before reading the new draft. Write it for that reader: concrete, complete,
professionally direct. No boilerplate gratitude, no boilerplate deference —
engagement is the thanks.

```markdown
# Response to review — <piece title>, v<N> → v<N+1>

- **Review answered:** correspondence/v<N>-review/ · **New draft:** drafts/v<N+1>.md
- **Author guidance applied:** <one-line summary, or "none">

## Flags and escalations (first, always)

<Identity-level decisions made this round and on whose authority: guidance
resolutions, conservative defaults taken under autonomy (each with the fork,
the option taken, and what taking the other option would have meant), anything
awaiting the human author. If none: "None.">

## Summary

<One paragraph: what this revision did — the two or three biggest moves and
what they were responding to. Then the counts: N findings — a accepted,
b adapted, c rebutted, d deferred, e escalated, f mooted by restructuring.>

## Point by point

<One entry per finding, grouped in the review's own order (majors, then
minors, then fact-check, discourse, line notes …). Format per entry:>

### <ID> — <the finding, verbatim where short, faithfully summarized where long> (<letter location>)

**<Disposition>.** <The response:>
- *accepted/adapted:* what changed and where — v<N+1> §/¶ reference, with a
  short quote when the change is textual; for adapt, one sentence on why the
  fix differs from the prescription.
- *rebutted:* the rebuttal, evidence first (sources by name/URL, or the
  precise textual demonstration), ending open: what evidence would change
  your mind.
- *deferred:* why not this round, and where it went (next-round agenda /
  future piece).
- *mooted:* which structural change removed the finding's object.
- *escalated:* the resolution and its authority (guidance / conservative
  default / awaiting author).

## New claims introduced this round

<Verified: count, with sources available in the draft's citations.
Unverified (if any): each listed verbatim with location — these enter the
next review round unchecked and are flagged so deliberately.>

## Unresolved

<Anything still open after the gap-analysis closure loop, stated plainly
with why. If none: "None.">

## For the next review

<Two or three sentences of genuine steer: where the author is least
confident in the revision, what the next review should press hardest on.
This is not softness — directing scrutiny is how authors get value from
reviewers.>
```

Rules:

- **Every ledger finding appears** — the Phase 5 coverage guarantee extends
  to the letter; a finding handled in the ledger but missing here is a gap.
- **Locations are checkable**: section/paragraph references into v<N+1> that
  a reader can open and confirm.
- Rebuttals never paraphrase the finding into something easier to answer;
  quote it.
- The letter reports the *outcome* of deliberation, compactly; the frozen
  ledger holds the full working record. Do not dump the ledger here.
