# Corpus Note (Phase 1)

One note per piece read, at `profile/corpus/<piece-slug>.md`. This is the
evidential layer: Phase 2's generalizations must be traceable to these notes, and
a later refresh reads them instead of re-reading the corpus.

Rules:

- **One piece, read in full.** Not an archive summary, not the first screen.
- **Observations, not generalizations.** "This piece opens on a misused word"
  belongs here; "the author always opens on a misused word" belongs in the voice
  print, after the notes support it.
- **Excerpts are short and purposeful** — up to ~40 words each, at most three per
  note, each with what it demonstrates. Never paste the piece.
- **Counts may be estimates**, labeled as such ("~28-word median across the first
  20 sentences").
